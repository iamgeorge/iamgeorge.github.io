Feelium: A Touchable Blimp Body for Aerial Telepresence

Wang, G. X.; Li, H.; Lin, S.; Wen, Y.; Hu, J.; Jin, Y.
Adjunct Proceedings of the 39th Annual ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST Adjunct 2026), Detroit, MI, USA
PDF ACM DL
Abstract

Floating things invite touch. We present Feelium, a blimp-based telepresence platform that enables visual embodiment and touch interaction through its inflatable skin. Through a VR headset, a remote person inhabits the blimp, looking out of it first-person, appearing on its skin as a face or avatar, and steering it through the room.

Partners in the room pat it, press a palm against it, draw on it, or lean into it; the skin senses each contact and renders it into the wearer's view in VR. Touch thus provides a physical interaction channel for remote presence, turning the skin into a shared surface between remote and co-located partners.
