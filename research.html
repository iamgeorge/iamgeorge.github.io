Research

I am a PhD student in Technology, AI and Society at Stony Brook University, within the College of Engineering and Applied Sciences, advised by Ben Zhang.

I study human-centered and responsible AI at the intersection of human–computer interaction and multimodal systems, with a focus on accessibility, spatial computing, and learning technologies.
